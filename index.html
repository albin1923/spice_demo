The Process

01. ETHICAL SOURCING

Directly from family estates in Kottayam, Kerala. Hand-picked at peak potency.

02. SUN DRYING

Natural curing under the tropical sun to concentrate essential oils and lock in vibrant colors.

03. PRECISION GRINDING

Cold-milled to preserve volatile aromatics, resulting in an explosive flavor profile.

The Power of Many

A synergistic powerhouse. The active curcumin in turmeric, combined with piperine from cracked black pepper, increases absorption by 2000%. The capsaicin from the chilies ignites metabolism, while cardamom acts as a potent anti-inflammatory agent.
